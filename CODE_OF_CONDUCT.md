# Community Code of Conduct

OpenSource Planet welcomes people of every background and experience level. Treat people with respect, assume good intent where reasonable, and keep criticism specific to work and ideas. Give credit, welcome questions, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and publishing private information are not welcome. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportional, explain the rule where appropriate, and preserve affected people's privacy. Repeated or severe violations may result in a permanent ban.

Report concerns to the maintainer through the public contact configured on the website when available. Do not include private information in public issues. If no private channel is configured, use GitHub's reporting or blocking tools and request a private contact without posting sensitive details. Maintainers should handle reports respectfully and avoid retaliation.
